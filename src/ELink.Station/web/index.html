    Telescope and scope

    A scope is a mount plus a telescope with its camera. This sets up the simple case: one camera, no guiding.

    A DSLR (GPhoto) does not tell its pixel size or sensor size: fill in all three from the camera's specs, e.g. 6000 x 4000 px at 3.9 µm. Cooled astro cameras report their own: leave them empty.

    Backlash

    Idle

    Slack in the mount's gears, measured by plate solving a few moves each way on both axes (a few minutes; the scope ends where it started). It is measured at the first image after the station starts; shots then always arrive from the same side on an axis with slack.
